About Sheela Sharma

Sheela Sharma is a Los Angeles-based actress originally from Detroit, MI. She appeared in several films, commercials, and stage plays throughout Detroit before making her move to LA. Sheela produces, writes, casts, and acts. She’s also a proud dog mom to two pups—Kitty (a Maltipoo) and Churro (a Toy Poodle).

About Reign Casting

Reign Casting is a boutique casting company specializing in vertical series, independent films, and diverse storytelling across platforms. With an eye for talent and a heart for storytelling, we help creators bring unforgettable characters to life.
